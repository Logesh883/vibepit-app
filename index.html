The Chat

Run Claude Code without living in the terminal

The Chat shows every Claude tab across your projects: what Claude is doing right now, what it has finished, and what it is waiting for. Work in several projects at once and still know exactly where each one stands.

Answer with one click

Questions with options, permission requests and plans show up with buttons. Give your own answer instead, or tell Claude why you said no.

Plans you can actually read

A plan opens beside the Chat, laid out like a document. Approve it, reply to it, pin it, or branch it into a new session.

Live progress

Watch Claude’s commands, the files it reads and edits, and its background tasks, with a timer. When it is done, it folds into one summary line.

Know it arrived

A message sent while Claude is busy shows Queued, then a green tick once Claude has read it. If it could not be delivered, the Chat says so.

Modes for each tab

Normal, plan, auto or accept edits, shown on every tab and changed from the Chat. Open more tabs in a project, or start one in another account.

Commands, skills and files

Type / to find Claude’s commands and skills. Attach files with the paper clip, drop them on the window, or paste a screenshot.

Links that lead somewhere

Pull requests, issues, CI runs, releases, commits, preview links and pictures from an answer sit right under its headline. A link to a review or a pull request’s changes says what it is.

History, branches and replies

Earlier conversations are kept by day. Continue one, branch any message to try another approach, reply to one specific answer, or copy a message Claude wrote for you to send.

Review PRs, run commands

“Review PR 42” runs a review with your own instructions. When Claude asks you to run something, it explains first and runs only when you confirm.

Voice

Talk to it when your hands are busy

Start with “Hey Pit”. For 30 seconds after each command, you can keep talking without the name. Begin with “Claude, …” to send a message to Claude, or with “Pit, …” for a command. Everything you can say, you can also type.

Your own words

No fixed phrases to learn. “Bring up abc” or “let it go ahead” both work. When a sentence could mean two things, VibePit asks, and remembers your answer.

Only your voice

Read five short sentences and VibePit learns your voice, so it ignores other people talking nearby. It keeps numbers, never a recording.

Check before sending

If you like, what you say lands in the message box first, so you can fix a misheard word before you press Return.

On your phone

Step away. Keep working with Claude.

Scan a QR code in VibePit and your phone’s browser can do what the Chat does, live. No app to install and no account to create.

Chat, answer and stop

Send Claude a message, answer its questions and plans, stop a tab or switch its mode, and watch each step Claude takes, from the sofa or the coffee queue.

Photos, voice, tasks and changes

Attach a photo, talk, see background tasks, run the commands Claude asks for, and see what changed. For one project or every project.

On Wi-Fi, or anywhere

On the same Wi-Fi by default, or from anywhere through an encrypted Cloudflare tunnel when you choose it.

Usage & accounts

Never get cut off mid-task

See where you stand with your Claude limits before they surprise you, and keep working when one account runs low.

Your limits at a glance

How much of your 5-hour and weekly limits you have used, and when each resets, in the Chat and per account.

Context meter

See how full each conversation is, so you know when it is time to start a fresh one.

Two Claude accounts

When one account nears its limit, VibePit offers the other. The conversation moves across, and what you send meanwhile goes with it.

Your week

The last 7 days for each project: how long Claude worked, how many messages you sent, and how long Claude waited for you.

Notes, pins and reminders

Pin key answers, write notes, keep checklists and set reminders on one board. Filter by kind, project or search, and copy any of them.

Suggested by Claude, saved by you

Ask Claude for a plan, steps or a reminder and it offers one as a note. Nothing is saved until you approve it.

While you are away

It comes and gets you when it’s needed

Start a few tasks, go do something else. VibePit tells you when Claude is done, or when it is waiting for you, and nothing else.

Spoken alerts and summaries

Hear “abc-claude is done” with a short, plain summary, or “abc-claude needs you”. Also as notifications, and inside the window while you use it.

Catch up

When you come back, a card in the Chats list tells you what each Claude tab did while you were gone.

Floating panel

A small panel on every desktop, even over full-screen apps, shows which tabs are ready, working or need you. Click to jump in.

Menu bar icon

Start or stop listening and open VibePit from the menu bar, with or without its window open.

The screen stays on

While any Claude session is working, your Mac does not go to sleep. When Claude is done, your usual settings apply again.

Reminders on time

A reminder still arrives with the laptop locked, because your Mac stays awake in the hour before it.

Privacy

Your voice and your code stay yours

VibePit turns your speech into text on your Mac, and a small model on your Mac works out what you meant. No audio is uploaded, and there is no VibePit account. The only things that leave your Mac are the messages you send to Claude, through your own Claude Code.

Local speech models

The Mac’s own speech recognition on macOS 26, or Whisper on older versions. Models download once, on first start.

Your own key, only if you want

For faster hearing you can add your own Deepgram key. Only what you say to VibePit is sent, after it hears its name.

Closed to the network by default

The app talks to itself on 127.0.0.1 only, and every request needs a secret token. It opens to your phone only while you have one connected, and only for that phone.

Project suggestions on request

“Set up with Claude” shows Claude your project names, top-level files and READMEs, only when you press it. Claude gets no tools, so it cannot read or change anything else.

Questions

Before you download

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 12 or later, and a Claude account for Claude Code (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise, or an API key). Apple silicon runs the speech models fastest. If Claude Code is not installed, the app installs it for you.

Do I have to talk to it?

No. Everything you can say, you can also type in the Chat, and typed commands are answered in writing. You can turn the microphone off completely and use VibePit as a window for your Claude Code sessions.

Do I need VS Code?

It is recommended: each project gets a VS Code window with its Claude tabs in the terminal panel. If you prefer, choose “Use Terminal instead” and Claude opens in Terminal windows.

How do I follow Claude on my phone?

Press the phone icon in the message box, choose one project or every project, and scan the QR code with your phone’s camera. Press Allow on the Mac, and your phone can use your Claude tabs in its browser. Connecting from outside your Wi-Fi needs Cloudflare’s free cloudflared installed on the Mac.

macOS says it cannot check the app for malicious software

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and press Open Anyway next to VibePit. You only need to do this once.

Why does it ask for the microphone and Accessibility?

The microphone lets VibePit hear you. Accessibility is needed only to open project windows in full screen and to paste dictated text. You can say no to either and use everything else.

Can I call it something other than Pit?

Yes. Say “Hey Pit, change your name to Friday”, or change it in the settings. Short, distinct names are recognised best.

How do I update or uninstall?

When a new version is available, the app tells you: press Update now, and your projects and settings are kept. To uninstall, quit VibePit, run ~/.vibepit/app/.venv/bin/python ~/.vibepit/app/install_hooks.py --remove in Terminal, drag VibePit to the Trash, and delete the ~/.vibepit folder.
