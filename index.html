The pit wall for vibe coders

One window for Claude Code. Hear when it’s done.

VibePit sits beside VS Code and looks after your Claude Code sessions. Say “Hey Pit, open abc”, and your project opens with Claude ready. Give Claude work by voice or by typing, answer its questions in one place, and hear when it is done or needs you.

Features

Everything Claude Code needs from you, in one place

Work in several projects at once without losing track. VibePit shows what each Claude tab is doing and brings you back only when you are needed.

Answer Claude from the app

Questions, permission requests and plans appear in the Chat. Answer them with one click, without switching to the terminal.

Speak in your own words

No fixed phrases to learn. A small model on your Mac works out whether you gave a command or a message for Claude.

Live progress

See what Claude is doing while it works, and know that each message you sent has reached it.

Your usage at a glance

See how much of your 5-hour and weekly Claude limits you have used, and how full each conversation is.

Notes and reminders

Pin answers, keep checklists and set reminders beside your work. Claude can suggest them; you decide what is saved.

Always in view

A floating panel and a menu bar icon show which Claude tabs are ready, working or waiting for you.

Install

Download, open, start talking

No terminal commands and nothing to clone. The app brings its own speech engine and sets the rest up the first time you open it. macOS asks once to let VibePit use the microphone: press Allow.

Open the download

Open the VibePit disk image and drag VibePit into Applications.

Allow it to open

The first time, macOS may say it cannot check the app. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, press Open Anyway next to VibePit and confirm.

Follow Get started

The first window guides you through the rest, one button per step: install Claude Code, log in, get VS Code, and add your projects.

Say “Hey Pit”

Press Start and allow the microphone. Then say “Hey Pit, open” and the name of one of your projects.

Voice commands

What you can say

Start with “Hey Pit”. For 30 seconds after each command, you can keep talking without the name. Begin with “Claude, …” to send a message to Claude, or with “Pit, …” for a command. Everything you can say, you can also type.

Privacy

Your voice stays on your Mac

VibePit turns your speech into text on your Mac, and a small model on your Mac works out what you meant. No audio is uploaded, and there is no account to create. The only things that leave your Mac are the messages you send to Claude, through your own Claude Code.

Local speech models

The Mac’s own speech recognition on macOS 26, or Whisper on older versions. Models download once, on first start.

Your own key, only if you want

For faster hearing you can add your own Deepgram key. Only what you say to VibePit is sent, after it hears its name.

Nothing listening on the network

The app talks to itself on 127.0.0.1 only, and every request needs a secret token.

Project suggestions on request

“Set up with Claude” shows Claude your project names, top-level files and READMEs, only when you press it. Claude gets no tools, so it cannot read or change anything else.

Questions

Before you download

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 12 or later, and a Claude account for Claude Code (Pro, Max, Team or Enterprise, or an API key). Apple silicon runs the speech models fastest. If Claude Code is not installed, the app installs it for you.

Do I need VS Code?

It is recommended: each project gets a VS Code window with its Claude tabs in the terminal panel. If you prefer, choose “Use Terminal instead” and Claude opens in Terminal windows.

macOS says it cannot check the app for malicious software

Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and press Open Anyway next to VibePit. You only need to do this once.

Why does it ask for the microphone and Accessibility?

The microphone lets VibePit hear you. Accessibility is needed only to open project windows in full screen and to paste dictated text. You can say no to Accessibility and use everything else.

Can I call it something other than Pit?

Yes. Say “Hey Pit, change your name to Friday”, or change it in the settings. Short, distinct names are recognised best.

How do I update or uninstall?

When a new version is available, the app tells you: press Update now, and your projects and settings are kept. To uninstall, quit VibePit, run ~/.vibepit/app/.venv/bin/python ~/.vibepit/app/install_hooks.py --remove in Terminal, drag VibePit to the Trash, and delete the ~/.vibepit folder.
